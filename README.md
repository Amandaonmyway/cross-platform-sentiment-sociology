# SocioLens: Cross-Platform Sentiment & Persona Mapping
> A quantitative framework to analyze ideological divergence and user personas across digital communities.

## 1. Research Motivation & Background
- **The Core Question**: How do different digital platforms (e.g., Reddit vs. niche forums) shape the sentiment and behavioral framing of identical socio-economic topics?
- **Interdisciplinary Approach**: Bridging traditional **sociological network theory** with **Python-driven NLP and data pipelines** to map modern digital echo chambers.

## 2. Methodology & Tech Stack
- **Languages & Libraries**: Python, Pandas, NumPy, NetworkX, NLTK / VADER (Sentiment Analysis), Scikit-learn (K-Means Clustering).
- **Pipeline Architecture**:
  1. *Data Ingestion*: Automated collection and cleaning of public discussions and metadata.
  2. *NLP & Sentiment Polarity*: Tokenization, stop-word removal, and sentiment score calculation.
  3. *Community & Persona Clustering*: Unsupervised clustering to map distinct user behavioral patterns across platforms.

## 4. Key Findings & Visualizations
- Comparative sentiment distribution curves and polarization indexes across sampled communities.
- Network topology graphs illustrating core conversational clusters and echo chamber boundaries.
